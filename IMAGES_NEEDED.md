## Recommended Image Specifications
- **Format**: JPEG or WebP
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1920x1080 for hero images, 1200x800 for cards
- **Orientation**: Landscape for destination cards
- **Style**: High-quality photos with good lighting, authentic Kenyan scenes
